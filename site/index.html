A small app in your tray that helps with language in every program. It fixes text typed in the wrong layout, translates, reads English aloud, checks your English and types what you say. All of it runs on your computer.

Every action has a key.

Each one works in every program. A key can be a combination, a modifier tapped twice, Pause, or a mouse side button. You change any of them on the action's page.

PauseKeyboard

Typed in the wrong layout? Keep typing.

Krya fixes a word typed in the wrong layout as soon as you finish it, and switches the layout for the next one. It reads the keys of the word you are typing, never the text around it.

  • Backspace right after a fix takes it back and keeps the word as typed.
  • Pause retypes the last words, or the selected text, in the layout that reads best. Again for the next one.
  • Learns as you go. A word you fix by hand twice is fixed always; a word of yours typed three times becomes yours.
  • Stays out of the way in password fields and full-screen games. NB and other abbreviations are left alone.
Shift×2Translate

Translate like DeepL. Paste it back.

Select a text in any program and press your key. The text and its translation open together; edit the text and it is translated again. Enter pastes the translation over your selection, and your clipboard stays as it was.

  • The direction is a rule. Your language goes into English, any other language into yours. 32 languages.
  • Three levels. Fast is Firefox's offline translator. Accurate and Most accurate run a Gemma 4 model on your own graphics card or processor.
  • Text on the screen. Ctrl+Alt+T, draw a box over a picture, a video or a game, and its text opens ready to translate.
your keyCheck my English

Write in English. The duck checks it.

Select what you wrote and press your Check key. Wrong words are struck out in red, the fix is in green, and one short note in your language explains the main mistake. A right text says so and stays as it is.

  • Real mistakes only. No rephrasing; code and names are left alone.
  • Measured on 44 texts, it caught 31 of 32 mistakes and changed none of the 11 right ones.
  • Your mistakes, kept. Library › Mistakes shows the ones you repeat first.
your keyDictate and transcribe

Say it, and it's typed.

Press a key and speak. Krya types the phrase where the cursor is, in the language you spoke or straight in English. It recognizes while you talk, so the text is there a moment after the key.

  • Quick on an ordinary processor. 22 seconds of Russian were typed 0.2 s after the key (Parakeet, on an Intel i9).
  • Transcripts of files, the microphone and Google Meet calls. Telling an hour's speakers apart takes about 2 minutes on an RTX card.
  • Words to spell. Names and terms come out written your way, in dictation and in transcripts.

Nothing you read leaves your computer.

Krya has no account, no cloud and no telemetry. The models download once and then work offline.

The texts you select and translate

Stay here

Translated on this computer; the recent ones are kept only here.

What you type

Stays here

Krya reads the keys of the word being typed and keeps nothing else. Learned words stay on this computer, and nothing is learned in a password field.

Your voice and transcripts

Stay here

Recognized on this computer. A compressed copy of the sound stays beside the transcript; the source file is never copied.

Your clipboard

As it was

Every copy Krya borrows is put back, and Windows' clipboard history never sees Krya's own copies.

Models and voices

Once

Downloaded when you first need them, from Mozilla and Hugging Face, each checked by size and SHA-256.

New versions

You decide

Krya never updates itself. You download a new installer from GitHub when you want it.

Three steps to the tray.

Free, open source, about a minute to set up. It installs over Altype and 2Talker and takes their words and settings along.

  1. 1

    Download the installer

    Get Krya-Setup.exe from the latest release on GitHub.

  2. 2

    Run it anyway

    The installer isn't signed, because Krya is free. Windows warns once: More info → Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Find the duck in the tray

    Krya starts with Windows. The first translation downloads your language pair once: about 85 MB for Russian ↔ English.

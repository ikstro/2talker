One tray icon instead of three apps.

The layout switcher, the translator tab and the grammar checker, plus dictation. Every action has a key; click a dashed cap and press the key you want, as you would in Krya.

Read

  • Listen to the selected textCtrlC×2
  • Listen to a piece of the screenCtrlAltS
  • Translate the selected text
  • Translate text from the screenCtrlAltT

Write

  • Fix the layout as I typeautomatic
  • Fix the last words or the selectionPause
  • Check my English
  • Switch the layout

Speak

  • Dictate where the cursor is
  • Dictate in Ukrainian, type English
  • Dictate a note into my notes folder
  • Record a Google Meet callAltShiftM

A key can be a combination, a modifier tapped twice, Pause, or a mouse side button.

Nothing you read leaves your computer.

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. And you don't have to take our word for it.

Open source, MIT

Every line is on GitHub: what Krya reads, what it keeps, what it sends. The speech engine answers only Krya.

Read the code

Stays on this computer

  • The texts you select and their translations
  • What you type: Krya reads only the keys of the current word, and learns nothing in a password field
  • Your voice and transcripts; source files are never copied
  • Your clipboard: put back as it was, and hidden from Windows' clipboard history

Downloads once

Models and voices, when you first need them, from Mozilla and Hugging Face, each checked by size and SHA-256.

Updates when you choose

Krya never updates itself; you download a new installer from GitHub.
